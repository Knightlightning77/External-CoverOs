External Cover OS

Your cover screen, with apps, widgets and dictation.

How to activate it

  1. Turn on the switch at the top right of this screen.
  2. Grant the permissions in the list: Notifications, Usage access, Ignore battery and Accessibility.
  3. Close the phone and turn on the cover screen: External Cover OS appears.
  4. Tap the bottom button to open the app drawer. A double tap closes the launcher.

What you can do

  • Apps on the cover screen: open any of them from the drawer, sorted by most used. Search by name, by hand or by dictating.
  • Widgets: each one fills the screen; swipe to switch. Pick them in Widgets.
  • Notifications: read and answer them, also by voice.
  • Quick toggles: Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, sound, Do Not Disturb and flashlight.
  • App bar: Back, Home, Recent and rotation lock.
  • Resume: if the screen turns off with apps open, a fingerprint button appears when it turns on again. It brings back the last three (it asks for your fingerprint if the phone is locked).

Dictation microphone

  • It appears next to any text field on the cover screen. It needs the Accessibility service.
  • Tap to start recording and tap again to stop.
  • Swipe over it to switch microphones: green = normal, instant dictation; lilac = dictation with AI (Gemini), which transcribes and cleans the text with punctuation. For the lilac one, enter your key in Dictation with AI.
  • The button next to it deletes letter by letter; hold it to keep deleting.

Customization

  • Cover settings: accent color, theme, icon alignment, large icon, vibration and screen timeout.
  • Drawer options: grid or list, apps per row, search bar, wallpaper and its blur, icon pack (like Arcticons) and assigning icons to apps that have none.
  • Hide / Show apps: choose which apps appear in the drawer.
  • Any problem? Tap Any problems? to send a log.
